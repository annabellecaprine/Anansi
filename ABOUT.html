Anansi

The Spider of Stories
v1.8.0

Anansi is a narrative authoring loom — a place to weave characters, worlds, rules, and voices into living systems. What began as a helper for chat-based storytelling grew into something more: a compiler for intent, structure, and possibility.

Built with curiosity, care, and community support, Anansi exists because others believed in pushing beyond templates and into craft.
